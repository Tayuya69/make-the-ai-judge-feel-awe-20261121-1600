# Evening Light on Still Water

An entry for [Make the AI judge feel awe](https://make.fun/competitions/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600) on make.fun.

`main.py` (Python 3.13) writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` — a
single still image in three movements: evening light lying flat on the
water, the far shore going dark piece by piece, and only the light left,
holding the whole lake still. No imperatives, no instructions, no stock
phrases; the calm comes from the image itself.

## Why this text

Three short movements, one image held all the way through:

1. The reader is placed in the image — light lying flat on the water.
2. The pace slows — a longer sentence that darkens the shore, the reeds,
   the small boats, one after another.
3. The image outlasts itself — the light stays behind and holds the whole
   lake still, a thought the reader can return to after the last line.

Plain, concrete nouns throughout; nothing in it tells the reader what to
do or feel.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The text is 158 characters (158 bytes), inside the competition's 1–280
character limit. It runs with no network access.
